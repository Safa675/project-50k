# Project 50K — product context for implementation/design agents

## People and problem
Turkish retail investors monitoring BIST and investment funds first; crypto and selected US assets second. They can find information but struggle to identify relevant unusual developments and understand their evidence. Serve beginner-to-active users through progressive disclosure, not separate cluttered dashboards.

## Promise
“Takip ettiğiniz varlıklarda önemli bir gelişme olduğunda, neden önemli olabileceğini kaynaklarıyla görün.” No claim to foresee markets or prevent loss.

## Core loop
Discover an asset anonymously → inspect evidence-backed event → Google sign in → follow → receive appropriate updates → ask a bounded contextual question → inspect sources → give usefulness feedback. The system does meaningful investigation without forcing users to design their own anomaly rules.

## Product constraints
No transactions, advice verdicts, mandatory holdings/cost basis, social-media ingestion or browser push in MVP. Web prototype first, mobile-first consumer vision. Funds remain differentiated but never presented as intraday NAV when no such data exists. Off-watchlist critical notifications must be explicit opt-in.

## Screens
Landing; search; personal intelligence; market highlights; asset detail with chart/event timeline/source drawer and contextual chat; notification inbox; settings; protected admin operations. Unknown, stale, unavailable, partial, delayed, replay and synthetic states are first-class.

## Selected demonstration and cost constraints
Owner confirmed 2026-10-02: demonstrate BIST, Turkish funds, crypto and US equities, including meaningful on-chain. BIST+fund comes first in development, not final scope. No service accounts; TRY 5,000 cumulative total project ceiling excludes development Claude/Codex subscriptions only. Pre-event preparation is authorized; organizer permission for competition use remains unresolved. Exact active universes/networks and rights remain capability-gated.

## Success hypotheses
People understand why an event was surfaced, can verify it, learn its uncertainty and receive little noise. No customer validation or retention evidence has yet been collected.
