# Product adoption and competition risks

Reviewed 2026-10-02 against v0.5 PRODUCT.md, documents 23/24, the research-lab limitations and current official competitor materials. **The largest product risk is an unproven reason to change an existing workflow or pay for another tool.** Reliable evidence investigation can be useful; combining alerts, Turkish AI and fund information does not itself establish differentiation.

This is a bounded desk review. No customer interview, hands-on competitor task, live alert pilot, payment, outreach or contact was performed. The selected BIST/fund/crypto/US demonstration with meaningful on-chain remains accepted. The tests below evaluate its value and comprehension; they do not authorize removing a market or changing advice boundaries.

## What the current evidence says

Fintables Evo explicitly advertises sourced conversational research using shares, funds, KAP documents and market data, fund comparisons, follow-up questions and calculations. Its product page also links an MCP route. Its package page lists fund tools, price alarms and AI. This is strong advertised overlap, including with a proposed source-backed fund/BIST distinction; it is not proof of equal task quality. [PR-S01](https://fintables.com/evo/nedir), [PR-S02](https://fintables.com/uyelik-paketleri).

TradingView documents watchlist-wide, per-symbol alerts with dynamic membership; Koyfin describes price, technical, valuation, news and filing alerts across watchlists with email/mobile channels. These establish that cross-asset monitoring and contextual alerts are existing categories. [PR-S03](https://www.tradingview.com/support/solutions/43000739708-watchlist-alerts-your-trading-edge/), [PR-S04](https://www.koyfin.com/features/alerts/).

ForInvest's published ForMind service terms describe natural-language financial analysis using market data, financials, disclosures and news. The separate ForMind landing page failed retrieval with HTTP 403, so it was not reviewed in full. Nansen markets labeled-wallet activity alerts and on-chain AI; Bubblemaps documents holder maps and transfer connections as investigative tools. Their accuracy, consumer experience and exact entitlements remain untested here. [PR-S05](https://www.forinvest.com/pro-plan), [PR-S06](https://nansen.ai/), [PR-S07](https://wiki.bubblemaps.io/bubblemaps-v2/how-does-it-work).

These pages do **not** prove missing competitor features or Project 50K superiority. Fintables' consumer AI/MCP access also does not establish rights to power our commercial product; its additional terms restrict commercial redistribution and unapproved automation. It is a comparison candidate, not an assumed licensed backend. [PR-S08](https://fintables.com/legal/ek-kullanim-kosullari).

## Seven concrete risks

Severity describes impact if the risk occurs. Evidence strength describes what is currently known, and is separate from severity and the product's heuristic evidence score. No universal numerical launch threshold is invented; before each study, the team should specify its sample, task, acceptable harm, time/cost constraints and decision rule.

| Risk | Severity | Evidence strength |
|---|---|---|
| PR-01 Weak differentiation and switching value | HIGH | STRONG advertised overlap; our comparative benefit UNTESTED |
| PR-02 Interesting information without paid recurring value | HIGH | LOW customer evidence; no willingness-to-pay or retention observed |
| PR-03 Alert fatigue versus missed important events | HIGH | MODERATE mechanism/specification evidence; live user burden UNTESTED |
| PR-04 Sources and scores create misplaced certainty | HIGH | STRONG logical/lab boundary; actual user interpretation UNTESTED |
| PR-05 One interface creates different cognitive burdens | MEDIUM | MODERATE scope/design evidence; usability UNTESTED |
| PR-06 Quiet periods and abstention look like product failure | MEDIUM | LOW adoption evidence; testable product hypothesis |
| PR-07 No demonstrated acquisition or trust-building channel | HIGH | LOW market evidence; channel economics and conversion UNTESTED |

### PR-01 — Existing tools may already solve the user's job

The competitor descriptions invalidate an **absence-based** pitch such as “nobody combines Turkish fund research, AI and sources.” They do not falsify the more demanding hypothesis that our proactive, low-noise investigation is easier or more useful. A user already comfortable with Fintables, TradingView or a specialist may see a second watchlist and another notification stream, not a replacement.

**Lean test:** observe the same consenting users complete five real jobs with their usual tools and an authorized prototype: find a fund; understand a specific unusual development; verify its source/time; set appropriate monitoring; ask a follow-up. Counterbalance task order, record tier, coverage, task completion, effort and mistakes. Ask which existing step or tool they would actually stop using. Do not score unobserved features as absent.

**Trigger/contingency:** if observed tasks show no meaningful benefit, retire the unsupported uniqueness/switching claim and improve the specific job that failed. Preserve the agreed markets; stronger breadth alone does not resolve this result. Long-term defensibility needs proven evidence quality, reliable dated datasets or distribution relationships, not simply a copied chat interface.

### PR-02 — “Unusual” may not be useful enough to pay for

An accurate volume, allocation or transfer observation can still leave the user asking what practical monitoring burden it removed. The product intentionally avoids buy/sell advice and portfolio accounting. Some prospective users may want precisely those outputs; adding certainty or advice to win them would violate the product, not validate it. Competitors' paid packages and free entry points do not establish a price our audience will pay.

**Lean test:** use a participant's last real missed development, including an ordinary comparison case. Observe whether the prototype helps them understand and verify it with less work, then invite an explicit choice between keeping their workflow and a clearly described priced pilot. A verbal “nice idea” is weak evidence; later authorized usage and actual payment/renewal are stronger. No payment or offer is executed by this report.

**Trigger/contingency:** if interest is mainly a request for trading calls, do not change into an advice product. Refine the monitoring job and intended buyer. If demonstrated value still cannot support a paid offer, keep monetization hypothetical and avoid pricing/revenue claims until the buyer/value relationship changes.

### PR-03 — Reducing noise can also suppress a useful warning

Personal alerts plus off-watchlist critical notifications can interrupt users repeatedly. Deduplication and cooldown help burden, but a blanket cooldown can also hide a genuinely separate event. The market lab contains a constructed separate-event counterexample and synthetic flag-burden comparisons; neither measures real user fatigue or financial recall. “Fewer alerts” is not automatically better detection.

**Lean test:** prepare an authorized chronological sequence containing ordinary days, repeated versions of one event and distinct close-together events. Observe which messages participants open, understand, ignore or mute and why; separately audit suppressed important events. Later opt-in pilots should track logical event counts, updates, useful reviews and muting alongside missed/late events, not open rate alone.

**Trigger/contingency:** hold a notification policy if its observed suppression hides a distinct eligible important event or if repetition makes the tested stream unusable. Fix event grouping, urgency and channel/cadence choices; use quiet digests for suitable content without silently deleting immediate critical requirements. Determine tolerable burden with the actual segment, not a universal quota borrowed from elsewhere.

### PR-04 — Polished citations and “78/100” may imply certainty

A source-supported observation can still be wrong, incomplete or unrelated to the price movement. The evidence lab deliberately retains a source-supported/world-false claim even with a heuristic score of 100. Typed claims and verified arithmetic constrain output; they do not establish source truth or causation. Users may read a high score as “safe,” “fraud confirmed” or a recommendation despite the disclaimer.

**Lean test:** show matched examples with high importance/weak support, low importance/strong support, unavailable support, conflicting sources and unexplained movement. Ask participants to explain what is known, what is inferred and what cannot be concluded, then show the original source. Record spontaneous buy/sell, safety and causality interpretations rather than prompting the correct answer.

**Trigger/contingency:** hold and revise the specific copy/score presentation if it produces a harmful certainty/advice interpretation. Keep score explanation, observation versus interpretation, source date, alternative explanations and abstention near the event. An attractive interface is beneficial only when it makes these distinctions easier to understand; it cannot compensate for unsupported claims.

### PR-05 — Beginner, fund-focused and active users need different depth and timing

A common interface must explain fund publication cadence, exchange-session market data and chain observations. A beginner may be overwhelmed by confidence components and contract identities; an active user may consider a simplified or delayed explanation unusable. Progressive disclosure is an appropriate proposal but has not been tested. The color lab verifies listed opaque pairings, not comprehension, glyphs, screen-reader flows or mobile chart use.

**Lean test:** recruit separately by actual behavior and observe the same asset/event/source/chat flow at mobile and desktop widths. Ask people to find the data date/lag, explain the alert, inspect its basis and return to their place. Measure wrong interpretations, blocked steps and effort before seeking aesthetic preferences. Test realistic long Turkish fund/company names and partial evidence.

**Trigger/contingency:** if users confuse fund NAV with live price, partial token coverage with total ownership or severity with direction, correct the shared contract/rendering and retest that flow. Change information depth and presentation, preserving essential source/mode/limitations and the agreed four-market scope. A passed contrast calculation alone is insufficient release evidence.

### PR-06 — Honest quiet states may offer too little visible recurring value

Meaningful events can be infrequent for a small watchlist. “No new warning” and repeated evidence insufficiency are truthful, but users may interpret them as missing coverage or a tool that does nothing. Conversely, filling quiet days with trivial alerts defeats the thesis. Whether sustained monitoring is valued between events is unknown.

**Lean test:** use a consented pilot long enough to contain ordinary and eventful periods appropriate to the chosen assets. Observe voluntary return, understanding of coverage, useful saved investigation and eventual renewal intent separately from notification-driven visits. Include an honest quiet-state comprehension task. No specific number of days is a universal retention test.

**Trigger/contingency:** if users cannot tell quiet monitoring from broken coverage, repair that distinction first. If they understand it but see no recurring value, reconsider the subscription proposition or present a useful permitted review/digest; do not manufacture alarms, inflate scores or claim false continuous protection.

### PR-07 — A good prototype still has to earn attention and trust

The package contains no measured acquisition channel, customer conversion or independent recommendation. A new information provider must give users a reason to share a watchlist and return without implying that Google sign-in or a polished identity certifies financial truth. Current Koyfin/TradingView materials also show mature alert workflows/channels; actual convenience gaps must be observed, not assumed. The responsive web MVP and mobile-first destination remain the product direction.

**Lean test:** later run separately authorized, transparent recruiting or landing tests in a relevant fund/BIST audience. Observe anonymous asset discovery, comprehension of the example and an intentional follow/sign-in step; distinguish curiosity clicks from successful repeated investigation. Compare the same grounded proposition with different entry paths, not fabricated testimonials or fear-based promises. Track team effort and any actual channel cost against engaged users, not raw impressions.

**Trigger/contingency:** if qualified visitors understand the promise but decline the follow-up job, investigate value/trust rather than hiding the sign-in behind pressure. If engagement depends on unsupported loss-prevention or crime claims, stop that acquisition message. If a channel needs economics beyond the approved cumulative budget, report the mismatch before spend; no distribution partnership or campaign is assumed.

## Hypotheses: what is disproved and what remains open

**Contradicted by current publisher evidence:** generic claims that watchlist alerts, AI-assisted Turkish BIST/fund research, source links or on-chain investigation have no competing products. An absence argument should not be used.

**Still untested:** lower monitoring effort, meaningful proactive investigations, fewer harmful interruptions, trust calibrated to evidence, better Turkish comprehension, voluntary repeat use, willingness to pay, affordable acquisition and a defensible advantage. None is established by synthetic lab counts, a polished logo, a publisher feature list or a rehearsed demo. Branding has a useful comprehension/recognition role; its time cost should be weighed against obtaining this behavioral evidence, not dismissed as inherently useless.

First learning priority: a same-job comparison and comprehension study, followed by an appropriately authorized ordinary-period pilot. Specify release-harm checks and learning decisions before reviewing results. All seven risks remain open until real evidence addresses them; no test or contact in this report has been executed.
