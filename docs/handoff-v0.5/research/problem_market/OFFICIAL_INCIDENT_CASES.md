# Official Turkish incident cases and knowledge timing

**Reviewed 2026-10-03.** Official records support a concrete information problem: investors must distinguish a fund's trading access from liquidation status, follow changing process announcements, identify reporting revisions and interpret an unusual-price disclosure without inventing its cause. They do not show that Project 50K predicted an incident, could have prevented a loss or has paying demand.

This is a deliberately selected **three-episode mechanism study**, not a random or exhaustive incident sample: the September 2026 fund episode, a reporting-line amendment and a price/volume disclosure with no additional company explanation. Multiple fund announcements below belong to one evolving episode. They are not independent anomalies to add together.

## 1. September 2026 funds: access, scope and changing process

The official 23 September SPK statement identifies **131 funds, seven portfolio-management companies and 455,758 unique investors according to MKK** [S01]. This is a cohort attached to a specified liquidation decision. It is not a measured loss, a count of fraud victims, the entire Turkish fund-investor population or a paying-user TAM. The statement does not supply investor-level outcomes or willingness to pay.

SPK's reported low-free-float concern and its fund-process measures support researching **liquidity, concentration, valuation and identity transparency**. They do not validate a detector threshold or identify unlawful conduct in every fund. Historical holdings, actual free-float snapshots, related-party exposures, contemporaneous prices and a verified public-availability trail were not assembled. The app could organize a received official announcement; it cannot infer undisclosed supervisory evidence or claim actionable foresight from a later explanation.

The sequence also separates information timeliness from transactional capacity: even a prompt alert after a closure notice cannot itself reopen trading, execute a redemption or guarantee a different investor outcome. No avoided-loss calculation or counterfactual trade was run.

## 2. AGROT: a reporting amendment whose stated aggregate impact is none

[KAP 1648456, Agrotech Yüksek Teknoloji ve Yatırım A.Ş.](https://www.kap.org.tr/tr/Bildirim/1648456) displays submission **12 August 2026 15:40:23** [S12]. It refers to a financial report published on 11 August for the January–June period and says values intended for cost of sales had been placed in another income row. The issuer states that the planned corrections have no net-period profit/loss or equity impact and that the accompanying report/notes do not change.

The form's correction flag is **no**, while its disclosure type/content explicitly concerns a financial-statement amendment. Therefore one Boolean correction flag alone cannot identify every economically meaningful source revision. The notice says an update will follow the necessary approval; this review did not verify the subsequent revised report or independently validate the accounting assertion.

**Mechanism and product boundary:** a line-item comparison could change ratios or classification while aggregate earnings remain unchanged. Preserve the originally received financial rows, flag the dated pending amendment, explain the issuer's stated scope and regenerate dependent calculations only after the revised report is admitted. Do not call the amendment fraud, a new earnings loss or a completed correction based on this notice. The financial period ending 30 June is not the public knowledge time. Prior to the amendment notice, this app would need the original report and validation evidence; hindsight cannot establish that it knew the line placement was wrong.

## 3. ISKPL: an unusual market movement without an established cause

[KAP 1634635, Işık Plastik](https://www.kap.org.tr/tr/Bildirim/1634635) displays submission **17 July 2026 18:42:25** [S13]. Responding to a Borsa İstanbul letter of the same date concerning unusual price and quantity movement, the company states that no undisclosed development requiring a material disclosure exists. This is an attributed company statement in a public record, not independent proof of the absence of every possible cause or risk.

**Mechanism and product boundary:** observed movement, a request for explanation and a stated lack of undisclosed company developments are three different facts. A monitoring app could combine admitted same-feed prices/volume with the dated disclosure and say the company reported no additional material development. It should leave causation unresolved. This review fetched no price/volume series, calculated no anomaly statistic, verified no underlying exchange letter and established no manipulation finding. Before the KAP notice, a market signal would require its own eligible data and prior-only baseline; the later statement cannot enter earlier features.

## 4. What these cases establish about the user problem

| Concrete information task | Observable difficulty in these records | Useful output hypothesis |
|---|---|---|
| “Does this apply to my fund or its manager?” | Closure, listed liquidation and conditional reopening have different scopes | Canonical entity/fund mapping with exact source scope and current dated status |
| “What changed since yesterday?” | Same-episode bulletins, clarifications and a correction flag evolve the record | One event history with explicit revisions and material-change reasons |
| “Is the change economic or presentational?” | A financial-row amendment may have no stated aggregate P&L/equity impact | Explain affected fields and issuer-stated effects; withhold unsupported conclusions |
| “Why did this price move?” | An unusual movement can lack a disclosed company explanation | Supported observation plus attributed response and unresolved cause |
| “Was this actually known then?” | Retrospective concerns, document dates and publication labels differ | Explicit knowledge basis and honest unavailable/unknown timing state |

These are **problem mechanisms and product hypotheses**. Official records document the event mechanics; they do not measure how many users miss them, how long manual checking takes, whether users trust the output or whether they will pay. Those need separate interviews and timed tasks using the exact records, with correct understanding and missed/false assertions assessed independently.

## 5. Case evidence is not anomaly frequency

No base-rate claim is supported by this selected set. The 131 funds share a defined regulatory episode; 455,758 is a unique-investor cohort; neither is a numerator for an independently sampled anomaly rate. A financial amendment and a price-explanation request also use different event definitions. Ten notices about one evolving episode should not become ten separate daily anomalies.

A frequency study needs a declared active universe and period, eligible asset/fund-days, feed/rights/coverage gaps, event definitions, exact source/revision times, episode deduplication and ordinary/no-event periods. It must separately count official developments, statistical alerts, amendments, updates and false/uncertain alarms. Loss, investor harm and fraud require their own defensible outcome evidence. No such denominator, labeled dataset, alert burden, detector precision or paying-market estimate was built here.

## 6. Timing and review admission

For all sources in this review, **historical `available_at` and app `ingested_at` remain unverified**. SPK pages provide dates; KAP pages provide displayed submission timestamps whose timezone is not stated in the reviewed content. Source-reported timestamps are recorded as raw metadata rather than invented timezone-aware UTC instants. Current retrieval on 2026-10-03 is not proof of the exact earlier first-public version. No source here is silently admitted as a validated historical replay bundle.

The 2026/54 PDF/index date mismatch is preserved, not resolved by choosing the earlier date. Retrospective 2025 observations remain September 2026 attributed context. No original archive, exhaustive fund mapping, point-in-time holdings, actual user order or private supervisory record was obtained.

This review creates only the two incident research artifacts. It performs no legal/security review, live provider experiment, new application implementation, email, spending or publication of a working product. The four-market/on-chain target, TRY 5,000 cumulative ceiling and unresolved G01 competition-use ruling remain unchanged.
