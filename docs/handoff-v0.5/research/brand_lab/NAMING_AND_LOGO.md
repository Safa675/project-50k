# Project 50K — Naming and Logo Exploration

**Screened:** 2026-10-02. **Status:** creative proposal, not an approved rename, registered mark, purchased domain or certified original logo. Project 50K remains the working codename until the owner selects a name.

## Recommended candidate: İzvanta

Turkish display name: **İzvanta**. International/ASCII form: **Izvanta**. Lowercase wordmark/slug: **izvanta**. Suggested pronunciation: `iz-van-ta`.

This is a coined product name. The Turkish `iz` evokes traces, monitoring and evidence. The rest is a creative construction that can suggest a viewpoint; it is not claimed to be a dictionary word or a verified linguistic derivation. Seven ASCII letters in the lowercase form make the URL/app spelling practical. Avoid translating it into a claim to predict prices or guarantee safety.

Suggested product line: `Piyasadaki gelişmeleri, kanıtlarıyla izle.` Alternative: `Değişimi fark et. Kaynağını gör.` These are copy proposals, not customer-tested slogans. Prefer the existing source-backed product promise for detailed landing text.

Why this candidate survived: it is pronounceable, not tied to one asset class, gives room for BIST/fund/crypto/US and mobile expansion, and describes observation rather than trading advice. The name alone does not communicate investment intelligence; a descriptive first-use subtitle is needed. Native-speaker recall/pronunciation and negative-association checks remain untested.

Opening `https://izvanta.com/` through the public retrieval tool was unsuccessful. This does **not** establish that the domain is unregistered, available, empty or inexpensive. The earlier RDAP attempt for a rejected candidate was also inaccessible. No domain purchase, account setup, handle reservation or filing was performed.

WIPO/EUIPO/company-register and authoritative registry/registrar results remain unexamined. Therefore the correct description is **“no clear exact commercial match retrieved in the public search scope”**, not **“unused worldwide”**, **“no similar name exists”** or **“legally available.”** Before public adoption, retain dated authoritative results and have the relevant similarity/rights question resolved. This is a candidate-screening boundary, not a legal opinion.
