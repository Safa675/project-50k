# Project 50K — Weaknesses, disadvantages and caution points

**Prepared:** 3 October 2026, Europe/Istanbul (the source-review UTC date is 2 October). **Scope:** the current v0.5 plan, owner decisions, completed offline laboratories and selected current primary sources. This is a risk assessment, not proof of business failure, measured failure probabilities, a legal opinion or a production security audit.

The main weakness is the combination of **unproven user advantage, unresolved data/usage dependencies and a broad delivery target under a small cumulative budget**. More engine sophistication cannot by itself resolve any of these. The plan already makes several sensible distinctions—unknown versus safe, evidence support versus probability, phased implementation versus accepted scope—and the risk is losing those distinctions when building or presenting the product.

The most valuable next evidence concerns actual usable/entitled inputs, one meaningful end-to-end experience in each required domain, measured alert usefulness and a bounded operating plan. Research completeness is not release readiness. No requirement has been marked implemented or release gate cleared by this assessment.

## Disadvantages that better code alone cannot remove

Fund inputs arrive at their actual disclosure/valuation cadence. Faster polling cannot manufacture intraday NAV or undisclosed holdings. Even compatible NAV/AUM/unit endpoints do not generally identify transaction cash, security selling or cash direction. The fund laboratory's paired ledgers establish this nonidentifiability under its assumptions. A useful conditional residual or dated composition fact remains possible; an exact-flow narrative needs additional evidence.

Public records and raw chain events can remain ambiguous. A transfer need not be a sale, and account/authority counts do not reveal independent beneficial owners. More retrieved documents can increase apparent corroboration without adding a new origin or resolving an incorrect premise. No general causal identification or source-truth guarantee is available from the current engine design.

A new product must earn attention and trust alongside existing workflows. The product intentionally avoids trading calls and mandatory holdings/accounting; users seeking those functions may not be its buyer. Quiet monitoring can be useful, but recurring demand during ordinary periods is untested. Adding trivial alerts to create activity would undermine the low-noise proposition. A polished identity can aid comprehension; it is not evidence of retention, willingness to pay or a durable advantage.

## Quantitative cautions: assumptions, not product measurements

[RISK_SCENARIOS.md](../research/risk_review/RISK_SCENARIOS.md) and the standard-library script contain 16 precision, eight point-burden, four cumulative-budget and four quota scenarios. All rates and prices are hand-authored, except the owner ceiling and the existing daily quota proposal. Fifteen arithmetic invariants pass and a separate rerun is byte-identical.

* If target prevalence is **assumed 0.1%**, sensitivity 80% and FPR 1%, population positive predictive value is **7.41%**. Reducing assumed FPR to 0.1% gives 44.47%. None of these rates was measured. They demonstrate dependence on the target's prevalence, not our alert precision.
* An authored all-null universe of 1,000 assets × 78 eligible observations at assumed 1% FPR gives **780 expected false point candidates/day**. This is not a market-calendar claim, notification forecast or measured event burden; episode, relevance and delivery policies intervene.
* With invented setup TRY 1,000 and reserve TRY 500, the TRY 5,000 ceiling leaves TRY 3,500. Invented all-in monthly burn TRY 1,000 yields 3.5 modeled months, not a supplier quote or approved operating plan.
* At the proposed quota ceiling, 100 users could request **30,000 turns in 30 days**. That is permitted exposure, not expected demand. Event investigation, retries and uncertain billable outcomes need separate reservation, while shared explanations need not be regenerated per watcher.

These calculations do not need independent observations for linear expected point counts, but dependence matters for variability, clusters and interpretation. Population PPV is computed from the specified conditional rates even with zero sample opportunities; finite-sample precision is a different quantity. Undefined population positive probability stays null. No scenario establishes cost, detector validity or user harm.

## Caution points for implementation and communication

Keep effective time, actual receipt, publication availability and revision/finality distinct. A newly recovered archive can support a new reconstruction; it cannot prove the system warned live earlier. Preserve failures and ineligible assets in evaluation denominators. Do not validate using only famous incidents, surviving assets, emitted answers or corrected present-day histories. Authored negative-fixture abstentions can be policy-correct; their proportion is not a production coverage estimate.

Measure source support, world truth where independently available, arithmetic correctness, completeness, timeliness and user usefulness separately. Keep heuristic evidence strength visibly distinct from probability, financial importance and price direction. An empty/failed source is unknown, not safe. The existing opaque color calculation does not establish understanding or rendered accessibility.

Treat source pages as untrusted data, keep tool/fetch destinations server-controlled and test real authorization/cache behavior. Persist exact revisions, eligibility and uncertain send attempts; retain correction visibility across timeline, conversation and inbox. Future personal-data processing, transfer, message and advice-like output classifications depend on actual flows/templates. No disclaimer, Google login, consent checkbox or refusal fixture alone settles those questions. Applicable existing G06/G07 and language reviews remain open.

Competition eligibility is separately unresolved: G01 requires an organizer ruling before competition use. Owner-authorized preparation continues. Proposed İzvanta branding remains uncleared and unapproved; name adoption is not part of the risk investigation.
