# Project 50K — v0.4 Substantive Audit

**Audit date:** 2026-10-02 · **Input:** unchanged Work handoff v1 / development baseline v0.3. **Output:** v0.4 audit revision of preparation materials. This is not an implemented application, empirical detector evaluation, legal opinion or production-security certification.

## Assessment

The package preserves the user's central intent: Türkiye first, mandatory BIST, funds as an anchor, meaningful AI investigation and asset chat, Google-only personal accounts, one watchlist, critical off-watchlist awareness, on-chain product scope, responsive web with a mobile-first destination, Turkish product language and deliberate human/Impeccable design review. The evidence-strength/probability distinction and many data-cadence/leakage caveats are sound.

Passing original checks did not establish semantic correctness. This audit found reproducible offline bugs, contract/specification mismatches and incomplete acceptance mappings. These have been corrected where the available evidence resolves them. The broad product scope has not been reduced or accepted as complete. No account, provider entitlement, customer traction, market accuracy, visual approval or external action is invented.

## Severity and status

HIGH means a fail-open, provenance or access/release issue with material consequences if adopted in an implementation. MEDIUM means misleading behavior, reproducibility or material contract/acceptance inconsistency. LOW means documentation/navigation/bibliographic precision. These are preparation-package severities, not findings of a deployed exploit.

`FIXED_LOCAL` means corrected offline helper behavior with regressions. `SPEC_CORRECTED` means documents/contracts now agree, with runtime enforcement still unimplemented. `GATE_OPEN` identifies external evidence or owner decisions that this audit cannot settle. Baseline line references below refer to the received v0.3 files; revised line numbers differ.

## Evidence that was confirmed

All 97 baseline files and original archives were preserved byte-for-byte. All 26 active numbered documents and86 active assets were read/reviewed, not merely inventoried. All 284 stable requirements and28 anomaly records were reconciled. Four event screenshots were visually inspected; their transcription is consistent. Full latest live form/schedule policy was not independently recovered, and competition permission remains unresolved.

The fifteen research works are identifiable through primary sources at their recorded depth. They motivate design rather than validate Project 50K. R01 body retrieval failed; R09 PDF text extraction was empty. Selected paper sections were read, not an invented complete paper corpus. The original SPK aggregate numbers are supported at the official source, but do not measure losses/fraud prevalence/TAM or prove early warning. No competitive absence/superiority claim is warranted.

Current official Next.js documentation did support16.3.8 on 2026-10-02. It was not corrected into an unsupported denial. Resolve current compatible versions at installation; no application lockfile/runtime was built.
