# Scientific and anomaly-method audit

Audit date: 2026-10-02. Baseline: `received/Project_50K_Work_Handoff_v1/baseline/Project_50K_v0.3`. The baseline was not modified. The accompanying coverage JSON identifies every file inspected and its hash; source verification JSON contains one row for each R01–R15.

## Conclusion and limits

All 15 research references correspond to real works identifiable through primary publisher, proceedings, author-manuscript or author-institution evidence. This is bibliographic and targeted content verification, **not** a replication, systematic review, historical backtest, model calibration, manipulation classifier validation or legal finding. R01 remains verified primarily through publisher/author search excerpts because direct retrieval failed. R09's author PDF was retrieved, but extraction returned no text; its metadata was independently checked on the author's institution record. R03's accessible author PDF improved its audit depth to abstract/introduction. R11 method/results/limitations, R12 collection methods, and R15 citation evaluation/limitations were inspected selectively; none is represented as exhaustively read.

The handoff already contains substantial scientific safeguards. It distinguishes unusualness from misconduct, states thresholds are project hypotheses, excludes test/future observations, abstains on zero MAD or deficient data, keeps heuristic evidence scores separate from probability, recognizes dependent signals, and explicitly calls empirical research pending. Preserve these controls. The package supports an implementation specification and synthetic arithmetic demonstration; it does not establish market usefulness or prevention of investment loss.

## Findings requiring concrete corrections

### SR-01 — MEDIUM — Symmetric fund-flow trigger has an outflow-only headline

Evidence: `docs/12_Anomaly_Catalog.md:132` uses `|rate|>=10%`, but line 136 says “Tahmini net fon çıkışında artış”; `config/anomaly_catalog.json:121–123` repeats that mismatch. A positive estimated inflow qualifies but would be described as increasing outflow. This is a semantic defect regardless of empirical thresholds.

Correction: use direction-specific inflow/outflow labels selected from the signed estimate, or use the neutral label “Tahmini net fon akışında belirgin değişim”. Keep the signed numerical estimate visible and preserve the end-period approximation warning. Verify the same-sized positive and negative flow cases yield the appropriate direction; NAV-only AUM changes must still yield zero estimated flow. For AN-FND-003, clarify whether the 10% investor-count trigger at line 145 means absolute change or positive change only.

### SR-02 — MEDIUM — The catalog does not distinguish method support from background/data references

Evidence: AN-EVT-001 requires KAP text (`docs/12_Anomaly_Catalog.md:206–210`) but cites S09, SEC EDGAR APIs (`research/SOURCE_REGISTER.md:53–57`; `config/anomaly_catalog.json:202–214`). AN-FND-006 cites the fund-flows paper R10 for holdings/HHI (`docs/12_Anomaly_Catalog.md:179–186`); AN-ONC-005 cites the pump-and-dump paper R12 for vesting/unlocks (`309–316`). R12's reviewed collection section concerns Telegram and exchange events, not a validated on-chain unlock detector (primary PDF, `turn31view1`, section 4.1). The bibliography's generic “Reading” label can make contextual works appear to derive the specific calculation.

Correction: add source roles per catalog entry: `data_contract`, `method`, `background`, `project_heuristic`, and `validation`. Add a primary KAP disclosure/interface source for EVT-001. Retain R10/R12 only as background where appropriate; document exact formula provenance or label it explicitly as project design. Cite verified issuer schedules and supported chain/program documentation for unlock mechanics. Do not imply the source list validates project thresholds.

### SR-03 — LOW — R06 mixes publication versions without identifying the journal record

Evidence: `docs/11_Literature_Review.md:79`, `research/references.bib:46–52`, and `research/sources.json:365–375` use year 2021 and “IEEE TKDE; author manuscript revised 2022”. The primary IEEE record/search excerpt identifies the final journal issue as TKDE 35(3), 2421–2429, March 2023 (`turn25search2`). The author arXiv page distinguishes its 2020 submission, 2022 revision and the ICDE 2022 extended abstract (`turn2view0`). Year 2021 can describe journal early access, so this is a version ambiguity, not an invented paper.

Correction: record the final journal citation as 2023, DOI `10.1109/TKDE.2021.3112126`, with `early_access_year: 2021`; separately record arXiv `2009.13807v5`, revised 2022-09-03. Do not substitute the separate ICDE extended abstract's pages for the TKDE article. Preserve exact versions in source rows and synchronize the four bibliographic surfaces.

### SR-04 — LOW — Bibliography loses publication structure and two author lists

Evidence: `research/references.bib` uses `@misc` for every item, while lines 111 and 120 contain literal `Patrick Lewis et al.` and `Shunyu Yao et al.` as BibTeX authors. The full names are available on the primary arXiv pages (`turn9view1`, `turn9view2`). This is acceptable display shorthand in prose, but unreliable structured author metadata.

Correction: use `@article` for journal papers and `@inproceedings` for proceedings papers; retain `@misc`/preprint fields where applicable. Store complete authors with `and` separators, use `and others` only for deliberately truncated bibliography rendering, and supply DOI, volume, issue and pages from verified primary records. Verified examples: R05 Signal Processing 167:107299, DOI `10.1016/j.sigpro.2019.107299`; R07 PMLR 70:1321–1330; R08 Annals of Statistics 51(2):816–845, DOI `10.1214/23-AOS2276`; R10 Journal of Finance 53(5):1589–1622; R12 USENIX pages 1609–1625; R15 EMNLP pages 6465–6488, DOI `10.18653/v1/2023.emnlp-main.398`.

### SR-05 — LOW — Source verification status needs per-attempt evidence

Evidence: the register is titled “Verified source register” (`research/SOURCE_REGISTER.md:1–3`); source entries record access date and depth but lack retrieval result, immutable reference/version, exact reviewed sections, or failure history. `docs/11_Literature_Review.md:7–9` correctly limits the review, but broad topic queries and a date alone do not make the search reproducible. A failed retrieval in this audit does not establish that the original author failed to retrieve it.

Correction: preserve the original review claim as a claimed prior depth and add this audit's separate verification fields: requested URL, fallback URL, retrieval timestamp, result, canonical identifier, version, reviewed sections, finding supported, and explicit failure. Distinguish metadata/excerpt confirmation from body-content inspection. A search-engine indexed primary excerpt is useful evidence but not a full paper read. Keep any later full-paper review as a separate release task.

## Research interpretation

R03 is a sensible challenger: the reviewed author abstract/introduction describes isolation through random trees/path lengths, not supervised fraud probability. R04 supports online run-length inference under its generative assumptions; R05 correctly belongs to offline research, and retrospective labels must retain their date. R06 supports skepticism about convenient benchmarks, but does not itself supply financial event labels. R13/R14 support retrieval and bounded tool workflows conceptually; neither verifies financial extraction or Turkish explanation performance. The selected papers motivate implementation choices rather than validate the product.

Preserve the handoff's no-overclaim language. Correct the concrete semantic/bibliographic/source-role defects, and keep the research gates open until licensed point-in-time datasets, annotation rules, prespecified comparisons, uncertainty intervals, and unsuccessful results exist.
