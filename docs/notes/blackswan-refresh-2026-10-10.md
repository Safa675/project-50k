# BlackSwan refresh boundary, 2026-10-10

Recorded before refresh computation. T5.1 uses the already registered all-funds-v2
protocol and immutable calibration, with unchanged detector parameters, code,
2,133-fund category and peer universe. No recalibration or widening is authorized
by the refresh. Newly discovered codes remain in the external archive for a
separate scope version after review; their downloaded files are retained.

New prices, including retrospective revisions, become eligible only at their
acquisition time. We do not infer an old publication time from a new download.
Identical current NAV/date observations are skipped; A-to-B-to-A revisions are
retained. Missing and ambiguous values stay unknown. Completed forward snapshots
are observations of their actual acquisition; no historical size/investor series
is invented. New KAP files are report indexes only, with no invented PDFs or
holdings. The original raw archive and sealed research bundles remain read-only.

Imports freeze verified completed file pairs and run atomically. Downloaders may
continue adding files; a later import picks up the additional completed pairs.
Real data stays local, DELAYED and rights UNREVIEWED. Every resulting detector
figure remains a development result, with no held-out accuracy or lead-time claim.
