## Interpretation limits

This is a post-event development analysis, not a blind prediction or measured
lead time. The 6 October catalog and categories introduce survivorship and
membership-selection limitations. The historical prices were acquired later;
their availability uses the documented publication assumption, and their earlier
revision history is not independently verified. The fixed calibration predates
the replay date, but the protocol itself was written after the known outcome.

An LLM can retain later knowledge from its training or prior conversations.
Scenario interpretation therefore needs cutoff-bound tools, separate chat
history, no later outcomes or status fields, and preferably model-local fund
identifiers. Date filtering alone cannot certify that an LLM has no hindsight.
