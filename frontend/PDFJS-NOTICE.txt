The pinned npm package supplies the locally served worker, fonts, CMaps,
ICC profiles and decoders. The preparation script copies their license
files alongside the generated assets; the full Apache license is served
at /pdfjs/6.4.299/LICENSE. These assets are generated locally and are not
downloaded from a CDN when a source document is viewed.
